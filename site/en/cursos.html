Registration & cancellation

Simple rules, so nothing surprises you.

Registration

Fill in the registration form and pay for your level at the first lesson, not in advance.

Above Beginner A1? Take the placement test and join the right group.

If we cancel

A group needs at least four students. If it does not fill, you can take individual lessons or wait for new students to enrol.

If you cancel

After paying you have up to six months to join another group. Missed lessons are not partially refunded.
