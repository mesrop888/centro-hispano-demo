A bridge to the Spanish-speaking world

Centro Hispano is an educational and cultural organisation founded on 15 December 2003 to bring the language and culture of the Spanish-speaking world to Armenia.

Mission

Excellent Spanish training from beginner to advanced and professional levels, for students from anywhere, at each person’s own pace and style, with a dynamic, modern and culturally grounded method.

We teach more than the language: its cultural, historical and social richness.

Vision

To be a leading school of Spanish as a foreign language, known for high standards, a personal approach and the ability to adapt, in person, online and hybrid.

A bridge that connects people, cultures and opportunities.

Our story, year by year.

Yellow marks the milestones: founding, the first courses, the new building.

  1. 2003

    Founded on 15 December by Armenian economists Ashot Parsyan and Vardan Asryan, with Néstor Martínez from Spain, Juan Carlos Flores from Ecuador and Carlos Antaramian from Mexico.

  2. 2004

    The first Spanish & Latin American Film Festival in Armenia, at NPAK. Later: a Spanish film series at the Moscow Cinema (2006) and a Latin American Film Week at Narekatsi Art Institute (2009).

  3. 2004

    The band “Son Latino” forms, with musicians from Colombia and Puerto Rico playing Caribbean son; debut at the Tigran Mets Hall of the Armenia Marriott.

  4. 2004

    “Tiempo Hispano”, a live radio show in Armenian and Spanish, the first of its kind; later “Corazón de Melón”, then “Radiofiesta”.

  5. 2005

    Spanish language courses begin, from beginner to the highest level.

  6. 2007

    Latin American folk dance classes with a Cuban instructor, and the Latin American Cuisine and Music Festival at The Club with Venezuelan musician Victor Rojas.

  7. 2008

    Brazilian Portuguese courses start, at the suggestion of the Brazilian Embassy, taught by Mateus Castello Branco.

  8. 2010

    The documentaries “Nectar of the Gods” and “Cuba in the Bottle”, broadcast on Public Television, H2 and ATV.

  9. 2011

    The album “I ♥ Latino”: salsa, bachata, merengue, cumbia and reggaeton.

  10. 2012

    “Aires de Tango” with Argentine singer Valeria Cherekian, and the start of the yearly Spanish Scholarship.

  11. 2013

    A master class by Colombian dancer Monica Conde, and the “I ♥ BCN” contest with a trip to Barcelona.

  12. 2014

    Acoustic evenings with David Rodriguez from Las Palmas de Gran Canaria.

  13. 2016

    A Cuban evening with a nine-member band, live music, dance and a master class.

  14. 2021

    Founder Ashot Parsyan is appointed Honorary Consul of Guatemala to Armenia; the consulate opens beside the Center (official ceremony, 23 October 2024).

  15. 2024

    A new building with a film studio, seminar rooms and event spaces. From August 2024 to June 2025, a monthly Spanish Film Forum with the Embassy of Spain.

  16. 2025

    “Olé”, a Spanish restaurant, opens inside the Center in August.

Ten reasons to learn Spanish.

Spoken by 7.6% of the world’s population today.

  1. It is the second language of international communication: more than 580 million speakers, second only to English.
  2. It is the second most spoken native language, on four continents, with nearly 483 million speakers.
  3. About 20 million people learn it every year, and demand keeps growing.
  4. It takes you to more than 20 Spanish-speaking countries, their culture and their food.
  5. Music, literature and cinema, in the original.
  6. It is the third most used language on the internet, after English and Chinese.
  7. Spain has Europe’s greatest biodiversity and is among the most visited countries in the world.
  8. It makes Portuguese, Italian, French or Romanian quicker to learn.
  9. It opens study at universities in Spain and across the Spanish-speaking world.
  10. It is a great way to meet people, make friends and grow professionally.
