# Surface brief: Centro Hispano site (all pages)

Scope: full static multi-page site (index, cursos, eventos, nosotros, contacto). Mode: Persuade (home, courses) with Read passages (about, policies).
Audience: prospective Spanish students in Yerevan + the Hispanic-culture community, equal weight.
Action: find a level and its monthly price, then call/write/visit. Secondary: follow events on Instagram/Facebook.
Proof: 2003 founding, real firsts (2004 film festival at NPAK, Son Latino, Tiempo Hispano radio), real prices, real photos.
Constraints: keep logo (red wordmark), red + yellow, existing photos; minimalistic. No backend: contact form posts nowhere; use mailto fallback.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Learning Spanish is one metro line. A1 to C2 are stations two months apart; the site is its wayfinding. Refuses the language-school default of a full-bleed smiling-classroom hero with a headline and card grid.

OWN-WORLD: White enamel ground, near-black type, one brand red (#D1303A, from the logo) that only ever draws the Line and primary actions, and one signal yellow (#FFC21A) used only as whole solid fields (a section, a station disc fill), never sprinkled. Barlow (a transit-signage grotesk) with Barlow Condensed for station plates; tabular figures for every price. Square corners, 2px/10px stroke vocabulary, no shadows, no gradients. Photos sit full-colour in plain rectangles with a station-plaque caption (date · place).

STORY: The visitor sees the whole route A1→C2 with monthly prices at once, understands where they would board (placement test above A1), sees the other lines (Accelerated, Passive, Conversational, Individual, DELE) as branches, sees twenty years of culture as the Center's history, then calls or visits Mashtots 52/2.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar: logo left, five nav links right, language pills. Left column: "Spanish, from your first word to C2." at ~clamp(44px,6vw,88px), one sentence under it, red "Find your level" button + phone number. Across the full width below, the red Line (10px) with eight station discs A1…C2, each labelled level name + "36,000 AMD / month" in tabular figures; the discs are live links into /cursos. Right of the headline: the classroom photo cropped tall.

FORM: La Línea (metro line diagram), position 3 on the ordered list; seed key 0cbfe3a2. Raises: colour at page scale (zine); ruled tabular course specs (wax-pack card); line state by stroke not hue: solid group levels, split sub-levels, dashed continuous courses (jackfield); every photo captioned with date/place (quilt). Signature interaction: hovering/focusing a station extends a thin tick down to its spec row; on scroll the line draws itself once from A1 to C2 (reduced-motion: static).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
