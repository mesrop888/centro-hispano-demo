# Surface brief: Centro Hispano site (all pages)

Scope: full static multi-page site (index, cursos, eventos, nosotros, contacto). Mode: Persuade (home, courses) with Read passages (about, policies).
Audience: prospective Spanish students in Yerevan + the Hispanic-culture community, equal weight.
Action: find a level and its monthly price, then call/write/visit. Secondary: follow events on Instagram/Facebook.
Constraints: keep logo, red + yellow, existing photos. NOT minimalistic (user rejected La Línea on 2026-09-26). Home page must show every site photograph (70 images; UI icons and logo files excluded). No backend: contact form composes a mailto.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The standing exit, taken by the user: the vibrant language-school standard, executed at the polish of Instituto Cervantes, Don Quijote/Enforex and Babbel. A sunny, full, photo-saturated site where the Center's twenty-two years of real people do the persuading.

OWN-WORLD: White and pale-sun (#FFF4D6) grounds with whole sections drenched in brand red (#D1303A) and sun yellow (#FFC21A), orange (#F47B20) as the third fiesta colour, deep wine ink (#2A1216) for the dark band. Bricolage Grotesque 800 display, Figtree body. Rounded 24px photo frames, pill buttons, rotated stickers ("Desde 2003"), brush-cut photo art from the old site as decorative cut-outs.

STORY: Visitor feels the energy (hero + moving photo ribbons), sees every course and price, sees diplomas and scholarships, gets lost in the fiesta wall, reads the history, meets the people, then calls or visits.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Red field. Left: "¿Y tú hablas español?" in huge white/yellow display type, one line of intro, yellow "Find your course" pill + white phone pill. Right: the beanbag classroom photo in a rounded frame, tilted yellow "Desde 2003" sticker, red brush cut-out overlapping. Bottom edge: first photo ribbon starts moving.

FORM: canon (standing exit), seed key 0cbfe3a2, benchmarks Cervantes / Don Quijote / Babbel. Signature interaction: two photo ribbons scrolling in opposite directions (pause on hover, static under reduced motion) and a fiesta wall whose photos open in a lightbox with keyboard navigation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
