## Platform

## Stack

delegated: static multi-page HTML/CSS with a little vanilla JS. The current site is Wix; a dependency-free static build is the most portable redesign deliverable.

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally (confirmed):

- **Prospective students** in Yerevan (and online learners) choosing a Spanish course: they need level, schedule, price and how to enrol.
- **The Hispanic-culture community** in Armenia: people coming for fiestas, concerts, film screenings, festivals and club evenings.

## Product Purpose

Centro Hispano ("Hispanic Center") is an educational and cultural organization founded 15 December 2003 in Yerevan to promote the language and culture of the Spanish-speaking world in Armenia. It runs Spanish courses from A1 to C2 plus DELE preparation, and organizes cultural events. Success: a visitor finds the right course and its price quickly and gets in touch; the community sees the Center as the home of Hispanic culture in Armenia.

## Positioning

Over two decades of firsts in Armenia: the first Spanish & Latin American Film Festival (2004, NPAK), the band "Son Latino" (2004), the first bilingual radio show "Tiempo Hispano" (2004), Spanish courses since 2005. Founded by Armenian economists Ashot Parsyan and Vardan Asryan with Néstor Martínez (Spain), Juan Carlos Flores (Ecuador) and Carlos Antaramian (Mexico). Classes led by local and native-speaking instructors.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Courses (prices in AMD, verbatim from current site):

- Classical Spanish: 6 levels, each 2 months, 3×/week, 1.5 h. A1, A2: 36,000/month. B1.1, B1.2, B2.1, B2.2: 48,000/month. C1, C2: 60,000/month.
- Accelerated: 2 months, 4×/week, 1.5 h, 64,000/month.
- Passive: 4 months, 2×/week, 1.5 h, 32,000/month.
- Conversational: continuous, 2×/week, 1.5 h, native speakers only, 24,000/month (8 lessons).
- Individual: 1–6×/week, 8,000 per 1-h lesson; two people 12,000.
- DELE preparation: Instituto Cervantes materials, 8,000 per 1-h lesson; certificate of completion.
- Placement test for anyone above A1. Online courses offered. Groups need at least 4 students. Cancellation policy: up to six months to join another group; no partial refunds.

Contact form exists today (name, email, subject, message); the redesign has no backend, so the form must be wired or replaced with mailto by the owner.

## Product Principles

1. Price and level clarity first: a visitor should know what a course costs within seconds.
2. Language and culture are one offer; courses and events share the stage.
3. Twenty years of real firsts are the proof; never substitute claims.
4. Quiet surfaces, loud facts.
